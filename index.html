Questions

Asked all the time in Park City.

How much does junk removal cost in Park City?

Most jobs start at $250. A full 7×14 trailer is $450 if you load it, $600 if we load it. Bigger jobs get a free custom quote on site — the price we quote is the price you pay.

How fast can you pick up?

Call or text before noon and same-day pickup is usually available in Park City, Kimball Junction, and Heber City. Text “JUNK” to 385-505-5205 with a photo and we'll reply with a fast ballpark.

Do I need to move anything to the curb first?

No. Point at what goes — furniture, appliances, hot tubs, debris — and the crew takes it from wherever it sits. You don't lift a finger.

Can you drop a dumpster instead?

Yes. For remodels and big cleanouts we arrange 15-yard dumpster drops through our sister operation — you fill it on your schedule, we haul it away.

Straight Answers

The questions everyone asks before they book.

“Why not just rent a dumpster and do it myself?”

You can — and then you load it yourself, on your schedule, and pay again if it fills up. With the Gone-Today Cleanout, the crew does the lifting, the price is locked before we start, and it's gone today instead of sitting in your driveway all week. The $600 we-load trailer holds roughly what a 15-yard bin does — without you touching any of it.

“What if the job is bigger than the photo showed?”

Then we tell you on site, before we start, and quote the real number. You approve it or you don't — the Firm-Price Guarantee means the price you approve is the price you pay. No mid-job surprises, no fees invented at the truck.

“I only have one item. Is it worth it?”

Single items and small pickups start at our $250 minimum — that's the couch, the fridge, the hot tub shell. If you've got a few items, send the photo anyway: most people are surprised how much fits in one trip.

No — that's the entire point. Garage, basement, backyard, third floor. Point at what goes and the crew takes it from wherever it sits. You don't lift a finger.
